About us

Tristan Devantier and Daniel Mantell

The founders of TriDan Electrical Contractors Pty Ltd, Tristan Devantier and Daniel Mantell, bought the RT Edwards Electrical Contracting Division effective 24 March 2016. Both are long-serving members of the RT Edwards community. They relocated the business back to Raceview in April.

Our purpose is to offer careful project design and execution in electrical contracting. We specialise in quality electrical, data and air-conditioning solutions for residential, commercial and industrial work. We work with contractors, builders, electrical engineers, Energex and the general public, and we aim to keep those relationships going from planning through to completion.
